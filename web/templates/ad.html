Directory sign-in

← Settings

People sign in with their normal Active Directory username and password (jsmith, CORP\\jsmith or jsmith@corp.example). Their password goes straight to your domain controller and is never stored here. Access comes from either a group below or an individual directory user added on the Users page. If a person has both, the individual setting wins. Local accounts always keep working, so you cannot lock yourself out.

Who may sign in: groups

Members of a group (including through nested groups) get that role. A person in several groups gets the highest. Use the group’s distinguished name or its plain name. People who are in none of these groups cannot sign in unless added as an individual directory user. Members only through their primary group (usually Domain Users) are not matched.
